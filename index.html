Common questions

Where is my information stored?

Minute stores your profile, activities, timers, schedules, and reports locally on your Mac. The app does not require an account.

How do I start tracking?

Open Tracking, create an activity, add it to your canvas, and use its play control. You can pause it from Minute, the menu bar, or the floating tracker.

How do reminders work?

Water and posture reminders are optional. Configure them in Settings and allow notifications when macOS asks for permission.

How can I reset Minute?

Use Reset all in Tracking to remove tracking content, or restart onboarding from Settings when you want to configure Minute again.
