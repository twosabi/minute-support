PRIVACY POLICY

Your work stays on your Mac.

Effective August 11, 2026

Overview

Minute: Workday Focus is designed to work without an account. The Mac App Store edition does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or usage data with Two Sabi Studio.

Information stored on your device

Information you enter in Minute—including your display name, work schedule, activities, timers, canvas layouts, preferences, and reports—is stored locally on your Mac. Two Sabi Studio does not have access to this information.

Notifications

Minute may request permission to show local notifications for timers, water reminders, and posture reminders. Notification settings and scheduling are handled on your Mac. You can change permission in macOS System Settings.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

The Mac App Store edition of Minute does not include third-party analytics, advertising, cross-app tracking, or behavioral profiling.

Apple services

Apple may process information related to downloading, purchasing, or reviewing the app under Apple’s own privacy policy. Two Sabi Studio does not control Apple’s processing of that information.

Data removal

Because Minute’s information is stored locally, you can remove it by resetting your content in the app or deleting Minute and its local app data from your Mac.

Changes to this policy

If Minute’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes take effect. The effective date at the top will also be revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to twosabistudio@gmail.com .
